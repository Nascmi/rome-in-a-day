# Historical Notes

Historical facts appear after a Rome district is mastered for the first time. They are short rewards, not required reading, and distinguish evidence from Roman tradition when that distinction matters.

## Date Style

All in-game dates use traditional notation:

- `312 B.C.`
- `54 B.C.`
- `A.D. 80`

The game does not use `BCE` or `CE`.

## District Sources

### First Settlement and Civic District

The Colosseum Archaeological Park describes the Palatine’s Iron Age settlement evidence and the reclamation of the marshy Forum valley in the late 7th century B.C. It also describes the Forum’s gradual political, religious, commercial, and judicial development.

Source: [The Roman Forum — Parco archeologico del Colosseo](https://colosseo.it/en/area/the-roman-forum/)

### Market District

Roma Capitale identifies the Forum Holitorium as the market area reserved for vegetables and legumes.

Source: [Foro Olitorio — Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/foro-olitorio)

### Residential Rome

Roma Capitale describes insulae as rental housing and explains how population growth and scarce land encouraged vertical construction.

Source: [Insula dell’Ara Coeli — Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/it/node/1104)

### Water and Faith

The Aqua Appia was built in 312 B.C. and ran almost entirely underground.

Source: [Aqua Appia — A Topographical Dictionary of Ancient Rome](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Gazetteer/Places/Europe/Italy/Lazio/Roma/Rome/_Texts/PLATOP%2A/Aqua_Appia.html)

### Senate District

Caesar began his Forum in 54 B.C. after the old Forum became inadequate. The project displaced private houses and relocated the Senate’s Curia.

Source: [Forum of Caesar — Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/forum-caesar)

### The Eternal City

The Flavian Amphitheatre was built in the 1st century A.D. The in-game note uses the traditional inauguration date under Titus, A.D. 80.

Source: [Colosseum Archaeological Park — Turismo Roma](https://www.turismoroma.it/en/places/parco-archeologico-del-colosseo)
